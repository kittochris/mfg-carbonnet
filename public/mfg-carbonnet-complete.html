📊 Financial Methodology

Base Assumptions

  • UK EV Population: 1.4M vehicles (SMMT 2024)
  • Average Charging Frequency: 2× per month = 24 sessions/year
  • Carbon per Session: 0.009 tonnes CO₂ (based on 45 kWh @ 85% renewable)
  • Carbon Credit Value: £25/tonne (UK ETS market rate)
  • Revenue per Session: £0.23 (0.009 × £25)

Revenue Distribution Model

Every £1.00 of carbon credit revenue is distributed as:

  • 50% (£0.50) → Consumers - In-store credits driving foot traffic
  • 25% (£0.25) → Retail Partners - Revenue share for hosting the app
  • 25% (£0.25) → MFG CarbonNet - Platform fee

This creates a triple win: consumers get valuable rewards, retailers gain customers and revenue, and the platform earns sustainable profits.

Market Capture Strategy

Leveraging Motor Fuel Group's extensive UK network of 900+ forecourt locations:

  • Year 1: 15,000 users (1.1% of UK EV drivers) - Initial MFG rollout
  • Year 2: 75,000 users (5.4% market capture) - Expanded network coverage
  • Year 3: 255,000 users (18.2% market capture) - Full nationwide deployment

With MFG's nationwide presence and established customer base, these targets represent achievable penetration across the company's retail network.
